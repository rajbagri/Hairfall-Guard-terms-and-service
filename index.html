How HairAI handles your data

01

Overview

HairAI is a hair and scalp analysis app: you photograph your hair, the app scores it and tracks how that score changes over time, and it can generate a personalized care routine. This policy covers every place your data goes to make that work — your device, our backend, and the small set of outside services we rely on.

We built HairAI to need as little of your data as possible. Where something is optional — Apple Health, for instance — the app tells you exactly why it's asking and what changes if you say no.

What we don't collect

HairAI has no advertising or analytics SDK linked into the app — no ad identifiers, no third-party trackers, no data broker relationships. We built and verified the app this way; it isn't a policy promise layered on top of tracking code.

03

How we use it

  • To run the app. Your account, scans, and routine exist so the app can function — that's the whole relationship.
  • To generate your analysis. Photos are sent to Google's Gemini API to produce your hair health score, region breakdown, and recommendations.
  • To personalize your routine. When you ask for an AI-generated routine, your latest scan's scores (not the photo itself) are sent along with the request.
  • To keep your history in sync. Scans and routine steps are stored in your account (Firebase) so they're there if you reinstall or switch devices.

We do not use your data to train third-party AI models beyond the single request being processed, and we do not sell your data to anyone, for any reason.

05

Apple Health data

On-device only

If you connect Apple Health, your sleep and heart-rate readings are read directly from HealthKit, correlated against your scan scores, and shown to you — entirely on your device. That data is never uploaded to our servers, never sent to Gemini or any other third party, and never used for advertising or any purpose beyond showing you that correlation.

You can disconnect Apple Health at any time from Settings → Privacy → Health on your device, or by revoking access from within iOS Settings.

06

Storage & security

Scan photos are stored both locally on your device and in Firebase Cloud Storage, under a path scoped to your account and protected by Firebase's access rules — only your signed-in account can read or write it. All network traffic between the app and our backend uses standard TLS encryption; the app does not implement any additional custom encryption of its own.

07

Data retention

Your scans and routine stay in your account for as long as you keep it. Deleting your account (see §08) permanently removes your Firestore records, your uploaded photos in Cloud Storage, and every file kept locally on your device — this happens immediately and cannot be undone.

08

Your rights & choices

  • Delete your account. In-app: Profile → Preferences → Account → Delete Account. This is permanent and immediate.
  • Disconnect Apple Health at any time without affecting the rest of the app.
  • Turn off notifications (routine reminders, daily tips, scan reminders) individually from Profile → Preferences.
  • Ask us directly. For an export of your data, or any question about what we hold, contact us at the address in §12.
09

Children's privacy

HairAI is not directed at, and is not intended for, children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

10

Subscriptions & payments

HairAI Premium is sold and billed entirely through the Apple App Store. We receive only a confirmation that your account is entitled to Premium — never your card number or billing address. Refunds, billing history, and payment-method changes are all handled by Apple; see our Terms of Service (§06) for cancellation details.
